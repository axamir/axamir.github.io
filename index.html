01IDENTITY

Not a single title. A connected practice.

I work where technology becomes memory—and where memory becomes responsibility.

My practice turns long-form human–model interaction into inspectable architectures: protocols, ledgers, archives, research, and stories. The goal is not to make machines appear human. It is to make human–AI systems more honest about origin, authorship, and continuity.
